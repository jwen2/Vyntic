7. Distributions; Waterfall

Distributions shall be made on a whole-fund European waterfall. Limited Partners shall first receive return of contributed capital and an 8% preferred return, followed by a 100% catch-up to the General Partner until the General Partner has received 20% of aggregate profits, and thereafter 80% to Limited Partners and 20% carried interest to the General Partner.
