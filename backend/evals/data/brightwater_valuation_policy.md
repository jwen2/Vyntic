1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes procedures for valuing portfolio investments held by Brightwater-sponsored private funds, including Level 3 privately held investments for which observable market prices are not available.

The policy is intended to promote consistency and auditability while recognizing that private-company valuations require judgment.

Purpose and Scope procedures are documented in internal memoranda, reviewed by finance, legal and compliance personnel where applicable, and retained in the Firm's books and records. The General Partner may modify these procedures in good faith to reflect market practice, investor requirements, legal developments or portfolio-company facts and circumstances.

The General Partner expects to apply judgment when implementing purpose and scope provisions. Such judgment may include consideration of timing, materiality, confidentiality, available information, administrative burden and whether the relevant matter is consistent with the Partnership's investment objectives.
